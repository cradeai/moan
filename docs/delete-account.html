How to request account deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, send an email to info@crade.ai with the subject line "Delete my account" and include the email address you used to sign up.

What gets deleted

  • Account information: Your email address, password hash, and profile data will be permanently deleted.
  • Authentication data: All session tokens and login records will be removed.

What is NOT stored

  • Location data (processed on-device only, never stored)
  • Motion/accelerometer data (processed on-device only, never stored)
  • Sound playback history

Timeline

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 days. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

Contact

If you have questions, contact us at info@crade.ai.
