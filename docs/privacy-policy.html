Privacy Policy

Moan ("we", "our", "the app") is operated by Crade Technology. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Last updated: April 6, 2026

Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and password when you sign up, or your name, email, and profile picture when you sign in with Google.
  • Location data: GPS location is accessed only when Drive Mode is active. Location data is used in real-time to detect speed and acceleration. We do not store or transmit your location.
  • Device motion data: Accelerometer data is used in Shake Mode to detect shaking. This data is processed locally and not stored or transmitted.

How We Use Your Data

  • Account information is used solely for authentication and account management.
  • Location data is used only during active Drive Mode sessions to provide real-time audio feedback based on speed changes.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties.

Data Storage

Account data is stored securely on Supabase (cloud infrastructure). Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Location and motion data are processed on-device only and are not stored on any server.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase - Authentication and account storage
  • Google Sign-In - Optional authentication method

Your Rights

  • Withdraw consent for data processing

Data Retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account. You can delete your account at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy
