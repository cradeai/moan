Vroomy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026 · Operated by Crade Technology OÜ

This policy describes what data the Vroomy app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Data We Collect

  • Anonymous device identifier — a random UUID generated locally on first launch. Used to identify your row in the leaderboard. Not linked to your Apple ID, phone number, email, or any other personal identifier.
  • Display name — the name you enter during onboarding (or that you set later in Settings). Shown next to your score on the leaderboard. You can change or anonymize it anytime.
  • Score and game mode (drive / shake) — your best score is uploaded so it appears on the global leaderboard.
  • GPS location — accessed only while Drive Mode is active, used in real time to detect speed and acceleration. Location data is never stored or transmitted off the device.
  • Device motion data — accelerometer readings used in Shake Mode. Processed locally only.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No email address, phone number, or password — there is no user account.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA).
  • No persistent location history.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.

How Your Data Is Used

  • Your anonymous device ID + display name + best score appear on the public global leaderboard inside the app.
  • Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing.

Where Data Is Stored

Leaderboard data (device ID, display name, score, mode) is stored on Supabase (EU region). All other data — including location, motion, and settings — stays on your device.

Your Choices

  • Stay anonymous: skip entering a name during onboarding and we generate an anonymous placeholder (e.g. Player_a3b9c1).
  • Change name: Settings → tap "Change" next to your leaderboard name.
  • Delete data: email us at the address below; we will remove your row from the leaderboard.
  • Disable location: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Vroomy.

Children

Vroomy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date and, when appropriate, notify users in the app.
